## What worked and what did not
- **Weight blending (WiSE-FT)** is the only method that kept T-Rex at 5/5 *and* moved the choice
  evals. `wise_0.15` matches CLM on T-Rex (shield on) and is at or above it on every listwise and
  tricky eval; general Q&A is within 0.1 points. Larger blends trade T-Rex away quickly: 0.25 → 4/5, 0.5 → 0/5.
- **Distillation** to the frozen post head at weight 1.0 does not protect T-Rex. Adding a strong L2
  anchor keeps T-Rex, but it also cancels every gain, leaving the head identical to the authors'.
- **Our ADP agent-data reconstruction** (`pipeline/build_agent_data.py` → `embed_agent.py` with
  `CLM_AGENT_LIMIT=4000`, 34 text configs, 118,465 train pairs) helps only a little (2/5 with
  distillation). It does not reproduce the authors' agent post-training.
- **Shield off:** every head except the authors' and the anchored copy drops to 45 deaths. Without the
  safety shield, the authors' T-Rex skill survives only when the weights are essentially unchanged.
  Report this next to any T-Rex claim.
