# Day 3 (23 Nov 2026): held-out tests, more seeds, AWQ

This round checks whether day 2's launch head (`wise_0.15`: 85% of the authors' post head + 15% of `post_choice_hn_frompost`)
holds up beyond the test sets that share sources with our training data. Modal spend for this round was about $5 to $7;
`modal billing summary` read $68.20 metered for all three days when this file was written.

## 2. T-Rex: more blends and 15 seeds (shield on)

| head | seeds | survived | deaths | mean best score | planner agreement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| authors' post head | 5 | 5/5 | 0 | 697.0 | 65.4% |
| wise_0.15 | 5 | 5/5 | 0 | 697.0 | 55.1% |
| wise_0.175 | 5 | 3/5 | 2 | 574.2 | 49.3% |
| wise_0.2 | 5 | 4/5 | 1 | 629.2 | 50.8% |
| wise_0.25 | 5 | 4/5 | 1 | 663.4 | 42.9% |
| authors' post head, 15 seeds | 15 | 15/15 | 0 | 697.0 | 64.8% |
| wise_0.15, 15 seeds | 15 | 14/15 | 1 | 685.8 | 54.3% |

Over 15 seeds `wise_0.15` survives 14/15 (its one death is seed 0, which survived in the 5-seed run, so real-time play is not fully
deterministic). Counting both runs it survives 19 of 20 games, against 20 of 20 for the authors' head: a near-tie, not a match. Survival is
not monotonic in alpha (0.175 → 3/5, 0.2 → 4/5), so 0.225 was skipped. With the shield off, every blend dies 45 times in 5 seeds, against 22 for the authors' head.

modal run innovations/modal_train.py --args "--root /data/emb/qwen3-8b-awq --chunks shard_05/83 --init /data/heads/qwen3_8b_midtrained_head.pt --steps 0 --out /data/runs/auth_awq"
