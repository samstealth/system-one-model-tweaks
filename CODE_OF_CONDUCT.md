# Code of Conduct

This project is a place to share experiments, including ones that fail. We want it to be easy to say
"I could not reproduce this" and easy to hear it.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Criticize results, methods and code. Do not criticize people.
- Back claims with something others can rerun: a command, a result file, a seed.
- Say what you do not know. "Not measured yet" is a good answer.
- Give credit. Much of this repo rests on work by the CLM authors and by Vizuara AI Labs.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal and political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their clear permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Any other conduct that would be out of place in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and review comments. It also
applies when someone speaks for the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

The maintainer is the GitHub user `samstealth`.

- For abuse or harassment, use the "Report content" entry in the menu of the comment, issue or pull
  request. That report goes to GitHub staff, in private.
- For anything else, open an issue and mention the maintainer. If the details should stay private, say
  only that you need a private channel and the maintainer will arrange one.

## What the maintainer may do

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break these rules.
Steps go from a private note, to a public warning, to a temporary block, to a permanent block, depending
on how serious and how repeated the conduct is. The maintainer will not share a reporter's identity
without their consent.
